    Inventory and prepayments are where a hardware company's cash hides. A container that is paid for but still on the water is an asset, not an expense, and it does not touch the income statement until an Alpha is sold.
    Customer deposits for the Nova are a liability. The cash arrived months ago. When a Nova ships, the liability becomes revenue and the only cash movement is the cost of goods going out.
    VAT payable grows every month and drops every quarter. In a growth phase it looks like free cash for ten weeks at a time, which is the classic trap for a young company.

    Budget tracking

    Budget versus actual, and the two paragraphs that go with it.

    A sample month with invented actuals, to show the format I would use: variance in euros and in percent, a flag only when both are material, and a short note written for the founders rather than for the accountant.

    Monthly note, October 2026 (sample)

    Revenue came in 9% under budget because 12 fewer Alphas shipped than planned. The gap is timing, not demand: the container that was due on the 14th cleared customs on the 29th and those units shipped in the first days of November. Gross margin per unit held at budget.

    Operating costs ran €8.4k over budget. Marketing overspent by €5.5k on the demo-drive tour, which generated 41 test rides and 9 orders, so I would keep it. Compliance overspent by €2.2k on RDW conformity testing for the Nova pre-series, which was always going to land in Q4 and should be moved into the Nova budget. Software is a small, permanent overrun from new Shopify apps and should be rebudgeted at €3.4k.

    Net effect on EBITDA: €17.4k below budget, of which €8.9k reverses in November when the 12 late units are invoiced. Watch item for next month: landed cost per unit on the late container, since the freight invoice included demurrage.

    Rules I use for the flag column

    • 1A variance is flagged only when it is above 5% and above €1,000. Small percentages on big lines and big percentages on tiny lines both waste attention.
    • 2Favourable and unfavourable are judged against profit, so lower costs are favourable and lower revenue is not.
    • 3Every flagged line gets one sentence: cause, whether it reverses, and what to do. If I cannot write that sentence, I do not understand the number yet.
    • 4Timing differences are named as such so nobody fixes a problem that will fix itself.

      What this is, and why I made it

      A CV says analytical. A model that ties shows it.

      What is on this page

      A single, connected model. One Alpha's unit economics feed the cash curve of a container. The container terms feed a twelve-month forecast that orders stock on its own. The forecast produces an income statement, a balance sheet and a cash flow statement that reconcile to the euro. The same numbers drive the journal entries. Change a slider at the top and every table on the page moves.

      Everything that is public, such as the €1,900 Alpha price, the €899 Nova pre-order, the 21% VAT, the Q4 2026 Nova release and the fact that assembly happens in Asia, is taken from selana.nl and press coverage. Everything else is an assumption, labelled as such, meant to be replaced by real figures in an afternoon.

      Why these modules

      Because they map onto the role. Financial statements and their practical use: the three-statement section. Bookkeeping, reconciliations and journal entries: the ledger and the close. Budget tracking and cost and revenue monitoring: the budget section and the landed-cost check. Analysing data to support decisions: the container curve, which answers the question a manufacturer asks most, which is how much cash one more container needs and for how long.

      What I would do first

      • Week 1Set up the bank and Shopify payout reconciliation so it runs in under an hour, and agree the chart of accounts for deposits, prepayments and landed cost.
      • Week 2Build the supplier prepayment roll-forward and the landed-cost sheet per container, with actuals against budget.
      • Week 3Open the Nova customer deposit ledger and the Connect subscription deferral, so revenue is recognised when it is earned.
      • Week 4Close the first month on the five-day schedule above and send the first report pack with a 13-week cash view.
